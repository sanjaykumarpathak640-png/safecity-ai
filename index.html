How Civic Repairs Directly Recalculate Route Safety

Unlike isolated reporting apps or static map pins, SafeCity AI connects municipal field action to personal safety scores in a verified feedback loop:

1

Hazard Reported

Citizen reports a broken streetlight or pothole. Route segment lighting/road factors are penalized.

2

Officer Verification

Municipal crew repairs the fixture and the officer uploads verifiable after-repair photographic evidence.

3

Safety Score Updates

The 7-factor engine recalculates corridor scores immediately with a transparent change audit note!

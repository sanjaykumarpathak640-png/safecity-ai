City Safety & Infrastructure Heatmap

Geographic visualization of reported civic hazards, verified emergency help points, and route corridor condition signals

Layer Legend

Green: Favorable Signals
Verified Police, Hospitals, & well-lit commercial zones
Yellow: Caution / Moderate
Level 2-3 reports: minor waterlogging or damaged footpaths
Orange: High Concern
Level 4 reports: non-functional streetlights & deep potholes
Red: Critical Hazard
Level 5 reports: open manholes or hazardous cave-ins

About the Data

Radius halos represent the estimated physical buffer impact of reported infrastructure impediments.

Exact citizen travel trajectories and private user identities are strictly excluded from public map layers.
Transparent Methodology Notice: Map layers represent informational civic infrastructure observations submitted by citizens and municipal crews. They do not constitute crime-risk classifications or predict personal safety.

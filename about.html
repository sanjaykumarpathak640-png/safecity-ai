About SafeCity AI

Methodology, transparent scoring weights, cybersecurity architecture, and safety disclaimers

1. Connected Civic & Personal Safety Vision

SafeCity AI bridges the gap between municipal infrastructure maintenance and personal navigation safety. Built as a full-stack smart-city application, the platform unites:

  • AI-Powered Visual Civic Complaint Management: Citizens report potholes, dark streetlights, drainage hazards, and sidewalk impediments with geotagging and photographic evidence.
  • HERGUARD-Style Explainable Route Recommendation: Recommends safer travel routes based on transparent, data-backed factors rather than opaque black-box assertions.
  • The Connected Feedback Loop: Verified municipal repair records directly recalculate route safety scores in real time, demonstrating measurable civic ROI.

2. Explainable 7-Factor Safety Score Engine

Each corridor segment is normalized from 0 to 100 across 7 transparent dimensions, aggregated across total route length:

Safety Score = (Lighting × 0.20) + (Crowd × 0.15) + (Incident History × 0.25) + (Help Access × 0.15) + (Time Factor × 0.10) + (Road Condition × 0.10) + (Community Signal × 0.05)
Lighting Quality (20%): Fixture functionality and absence of dark blackout spans. 0.20
Incident-History Signal (25%): Historical civic incident reports and safety frequency index. 0.25
Crowd Activity (15%): Pedestrian flow, open retail businesses, and transit hub proximity. 0.15
Proximity to Help Points (15%): Distance buffer to police stations, 24/7 hospitals, and kiosks. 0.15
Time-Related Factor (10%): Dynamic adjustment for daylight vs late-night isolation. 0.10
Road Condition (10%): Asphalt smoothness, sidewalk integrity, and absence of open potholes. 0.10
Approved Community Reports (5%): Moderated resident reviews and star assessments. 0.05

3. Cybersecurity & Data Privacy Standards

  • Prepared Statements: 100% of database queries use PDO parameterized queries, preventing SQL injection.
  • Password Protection: Passwords hashed with standard password_hash(PASSWORD_BCRYPT).
  • Session Hardening: Secure PHP sessions with httponly, SameSite=Lax, and session ID regeneration.
  • Scrubbed Public Tracking: The public php/get_status.php endpoint strips citizen email, phone, and account details.
  • Upload Sanitization: MIME-type verification with finfo, cryptographically safe random filenames, size limits, and script execution prevention via uploads/.htaccess.
  • Temporary Share Tokens: Safe Journey share links use 192-bit secure random tokens that expire in 3 hours and can be revoked at any time.
Official Safety Disclaimer

SafeCity AI is an engineering and civic demonstration prototype. Safety scores and classifications represent relative reported infrastructure conditions, illumination quality, and civic maintenance status.

  • The system does NOT predict criminality, identify suspects, or evaluate individuals.
  • A higher score indicates more favorable reported infrastructure signals; it does NOT guarantee personal safety or immune travel.
  • Logging an SOS alert in this database does NOT dispatch emergency services. In any real emergency, immediately dial 112 or 100.
